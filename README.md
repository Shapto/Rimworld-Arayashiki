# Arayashiki

Tiansha Star's Blade, formally known as Arayashiki, is a Relic weapon from Limbus Company. It inflicts cuts that can never be made whole again. I did not like the state of RimWorld modding for my favorite Relic so I made my own. I tried to be as lore accurate as I could. Primarily made for personal use to cause hype moments & aura in my save. My first published mod!

---

## Description

The weapon has two modes: Drawn and sheathed. Sheathed is just a run of the mill blunt weapon whereas unsheathed is where the fun begins.

Arayashiki inflicts ludicrous damage, slices through cataphract armor like butter, deals 10x more damage to buildings and has a 0.6s attack cooldown. Every 4th hit activates a move called 'Brushstroke' which acts like 1 + 2 free bonus attacks that happen in quick succession after. Melee attacks inflict a hediff called Sizzling wound. It is a wound that will never reach 0 severity on its own and getting within 8 tiles of Arayashiki (either version) will reopen them back to their original severity while also untending them.

This weapon has 2 abilities:

### Slay the Heavens - Tiansha
Medium memory cost. 6 tiles of range, the wielder dashes towards a target, does a cool animation and finishes with an attack that instantly kills the target. 30s cooldown.

### 'Erasing me, Erasing you'
GIGANTIC memory cost. 12 tiles of range, the wielder dashes towards a target, does an even cooler animation and finishes with an attack that OBLITERATES the target. Has special logic that erases the target from memory.

Every swing while unsheathed mangles the wielder's memories. It erases their memories first (Precious memories > Opinions > Mood), then skills, then traits, working down to actively erasing their relationships (Parent, Lover, Daughter) until it starts severing the pawn's Melee skill.

---

## Obtainment

Arayashiki appears as a rare quest reward, the same way the vanometric cell or other rare objects do. It's valued at $10000. It cannot be crafted, bought or melted down. It is also obtainable via Ideology relic quests.

## Compatibility

**CE?**
I did not make a patch as I am an avid user of Yayo's Combat 3.

**Melee Animation?**
Patched.

It should be very compatible by default, but I am unsure if mods that change the pawn opinion system too much will have it erase correctly.

As usual, try not to remove this mod mid-save, as it is complex — but it should be safe to add.
